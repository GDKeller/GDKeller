# Building products & experiences where human and machine meet

15+ years building frontend and full-stack software, with side projects in embedded and desktop. Led the Accessibility Community of Practice at Alley Interactive driving WCAG compliance across the org, and architected systems for editorial products at New York Post scale (390M+ monthly pageviews).


## Work

**Current:** Lead Software Engineer at [Davant Systems](https://davantsystems.com)  
Building AI-powered creative software for visual arts professionals & [photobooth experiences](https://www.davantsystems.com/photobooth/) for clients including Ralph Lauren, AMD, UCLA, and ASUS.

**Previous clients & employers:**
NASA JPL, [Natural History Museum of LA](https://nhm.org/anglerfish), [Alley Interactive](https://alley.com/) (New York Post), [Studio III](https://www.studio3marketing.com/), Keller Digital (LA Dept. of Public Health, Dun & Bradstreet), and others.


## Stack

- **AI:** Claude Code · Stable Diffusion · Local LLMs
